Universal Heuristic Legitimate government fellowships, verified employers, and accredited universities will never solicit processing fees before disbursement.
REF #GUIDE-SCH-01
High Risk Target

Fake scholarship

Sophisticated spoofed portals replicating the National Scholarship Portal to harvest applicant credentials and collect processing levies.

How It Works 4 PHASES
1
Unsolicited Alert Target receives an SMS or WhatsApp notifying them of a provisional ₹75,000 scholarship approval.
2
Cloned Government Portal Link redirects to an exact visual clone of official portals hosted on a deceptive top-level domain.
3
Credential & KYC Harvest Applicant submits Aadhaar, PAN, academic marksheets, and bank account credentials for "file validation".
4
Mandatory Clearance Fee A demand of ₹499 to ₹2,500 is made via personal UPI VPA for refundable disbursement insurance.
Red Flags 5 INDICATORS
URL ends in .org-portal.in, .scholarship-gov.co instead of genuine .gov.in.
Guaranteed award sanction without requiring formal merit rank or verifiable income criteria documentation.
Payment directed to an individual UPI handle (e.g., admin.nsp@oksbi) instead of a verified treasury gateway.
Artificial urgency claiming disbursement allocation lapses within 2 hours if fee remains unpaid.
Official state emblem or ministry seal reproduced with blurry compression artifacts.
What To Do ACTION CHECKLIST
1
Halt Transaction Immediately Never transmit security or processing charges. Authorized public schemes do not request money to give money.
2
Cross-Check Top-Level Domain Verify that the exact browser host ends with the strict institutional suffix .gov.in or .nic.in.
3
Institute Nodal Officer Verification Contact your university administrative scholarship desk to validate if the notified scheme exists.
4
Report to National Cyber Portal File an incident report via 1930 helpline or cybercrime.gov.in with domain and payment handle screenshots.
