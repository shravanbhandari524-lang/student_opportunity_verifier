Fake scholarship

HIGH INCIDENCE

Scams demanding upfront processing charges for bogus student grants.

HOW IT WORKS 4-STAGE SEQUENCE
01. Unsolicited notification · Message claims selection for national merit award.
DISPATCH
02. Authority spoofing · Forged emblem, letterhead or spoofed ministry domain.
FORGERY
03. Clearance demand · Non-refundable verification charge demanded via UPI.
EXTORTION
04. Disappearance · Sender vanishes after transaction is confirmed.
SEVERANCE
RED FLAGS RISK INDICATORS
Upfront verification or document stamp fee requested.
Personal UPI handle or savings account used instead of treasury portal.
Newly registered domain with hyphenated official keywords.
Urgency pressure demanding payment within 24 hours to secure seat.
Selection granted without application or verifiable institutional roll number.
WHAT TO DO COUNTERMEASURES
01. Verify officially · Cross-reference directly on National Scholarship Portal.
